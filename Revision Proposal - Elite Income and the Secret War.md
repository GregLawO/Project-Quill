# Revision Proposal: Elite Income, the Secret War, and the Staged Realization

**Status.** A proposal for the author's approval. Nothing in the outline, the bible files, the profiles, or the drafts has been changed. Every item below is an author ruling from the 2 November 2026 discussion unless it is marked (decision) or (open).

## 1. Rulings to record

1. **Order of the foundation (author ruling).**
   1. Kenji realizes that selling his inventions at once would make many enemies, and decides to build a safety net first.
   2. He builds a Level 2 Med-Pod that restores a dying person to full health and also rejuvenates and extends life.
   3. He recruits veterans and experts in combat and other fields. Each serves him for a set number of years in exchange for being restored to their prime.
   4. Those recruits become a secret organization that protects him.
   5. When it is ready, he offers the same treatment to the world's elites, to win favor and build influence so he can build his base without much trouble.
   6. In 2053 a subordinate asks whether the treatment can save his dying adoptive father, Tom Shepard. This is how Kenji meets the Shepards.
   7. Over the following years he grows the organization, begins his relationship with Hannah, and marries her in 2058.
   8. After the wedding, he slowly and secretly spreads his inventions through legal and shell companies.
2. **Income (author ruling).** His main income is the elites who pay heavily for Level 2 rejuvenation and longevity. That money funds the public Primitive-grade clinics. The published A1 stands: public clinics, with queues of families in 2058 and a licensing department closing a district clinic in 2059.
3. **Security (author ruling).** His men wear Level 2 Mobile Infantry Powered Suits. His droids are Level 3, driven by Dumb AI running on a data crystal system that nobody at the time can hack. No one who knows of him would risk their long life by fighting him, because their follow-up treatments come only through him. The one exception is Cerberus.
4. **Limits of protection (author ruling).** Good protection does not guarantee safety. A dedicated enemy willing to die can still wound or kill him and the people he loves. The 2059 bomb stands.
5. **The theft (author ruling).** Cerberus steals a batch of five Level 2 Med-Pods. It cannot reproduce them, because it lacks the knowledge and expertise in Med-Pod and data crystal technology.
6. **The secret war (author ruling).** Cerberus is identified much earlier than the bible says. It hears of the technology that restores a person to their prime and starts digging. It finally traces the inventions back to him after they begin to spread, and that is the start of a secret war between shadow organizations.
7. **Tom's adopted son (author ruling).** Tom is the dying man. The subordinate who brings him is Tom's adopted son. The adopted son dies in the 2059 bombing, for more impact.
8. **The realization comes in three waves (author ruling).**
   1. A small list of companies with very familiar names.
   2. The Alpha Centauri expedition announcement (Manswell, 2065), when he understands why the names were familiar.
   3. The Mars cavern, uncovered by his droids in 2065, which is the final and irrevocable confirmation that he is in the Mass Effect universe.

## 2. What the published A1 and the bible already allow

- **Wave 3 is already canon.** Protagonist and Timeline, Section 5, has the network's droid crews finding the Mars cavern in 2065, and the 2065 row of the timeline already lists the discovery. The cavern is on Mars. Earlier in the discussion I wrongly said it was at Charon.
- **Wave 2 is already canon.** Section 3 has the Manswell announcement in 2065, when "the organization names he has been meeting fall into place."
- **Wave 1 is new in how it is shown.** The bible has the names falling into place in 2065, but no earlier moment where he notices them. Before 2065 Kenji's profile allows "no thought that this was once a story." (decision) Wave 1 is a feeling of recognition he cannot place, with no story thought and no Reaper thought.
- **The 2053 meeting is a change.** A2.5 has Kenji choose Tom's file himself, after watching Hannah on the monitors.
- **The 2059 bomb scene in A1 limits where the adopted son can die.** Hannah checks the staff roster on the lawn and finds all of them there ("All of them. All there."). Her scene also notes the Thursday delivery van has not come, and the gate stool is empty with the guard's coffee cup gone. The adopted son cannot be among the staff on that lawn.

## 3. Proposed handling (decisions)

- **The adopted son's death (decision).** He is the gate guard whose stool is empty. He left his post to go after the delivery van that did not come, and died outside the building. This fits the published scene ("small absences, each with a reasonable excuse"), the roster stays true, and the absence Hannah noticed becomes the reason he is missed. Name and age are open.
- **Tom's "son" (decision).** Tom first calls Kenji "son" in 2053 in the drafted A2.5. If Tom already has an adopted son, the word means more, and it could change who "son" is aimed at after 2059. Proposal: keep the 2053 line, and after 2059 Tom calls Kenji "son" with the new weight. Kenji's "Sir" and "Ma'am" until the wedding, and "Dad" and "Mom" afterward, are unchanged.
- **Term of service and dependence (decision).** Recruits serve a set number of years for their restoration. The follow-up treatments, which only his network can give, keep them loyal beyond the term, and the conditioning layers stay as written in item 3 of the timeline.
- **The hardware dates (open).** When the Level 2 suits, the Level 3 droids, and the data crystal system first appear, and whether they are in place before the elite rollout. The drafted A2.3 to A2.10 currently show Level 2 sentries (loud), Level 1 controllers, and the first data crystal bank in 2060.
- **The pod theft (open).** Date, method, and how long five Level 2 pods last without maintenance. The drafted A2.4 has one stolen Primitive pod in 2061.
- **The secret war (open).** When Cerberus is first identified, what the network knows of it before 2065, and how it still reaches the 2059 bomb and the 2065 withdrawals if Kenji's side is already looking for it.
