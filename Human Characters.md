# Human_Characters.md

**Purpose.** The roster for the whole cast. It holds what no single profile can: story-wide rulings, the age and role-fit tables, relationships and secrets between characters, the POV roster, and full entries for every character who has no profile of their own. Characters with a profile (Kenji, Hannah, Brooks, Manswell, Tom, Ruth, Danny, Vance, Ashby, Byron, Marchetti, Whitcombe) get one pointer line here, and the profile governs their voice, habits, appearance, and personal facts. Nothing a profile already says is repeated in this file.

**Scope of this copy.** This file integrates the two drop-in updates (the first, and the 1 November 2026 Act I revision) into one standalone document. The original full file is not in the project, so its other rulings (2, 4, 6 to 10, 12 to 14, 16, 18, 22 to 24), the entries for characters not named here (Harper, Anderson, Hackett, Grissom, Zaeed, Udina, and the rest), Section 8A's recurring OCs, and the full Section 10 are not reproduced. Those stay in the original and should be merged in. Decisions that the author left to me are marked (decision).

## 1. Rulings

**1. The Protagonist.** His name is **Kenji Himura**, of Japanese descent. His body is thirty in 2050 (born 2020). He marries Hannah in 2058.

**3. Alexandra's birth.** In 2059, five months pregnant, Hannah survives a bomb at the Galveston clinic complex, but the child she carries is lost. The attack was carried out for the conspiracy (ruling 28), though it was not the conspiracy's first move, since its first moves date from 2055 (ruling 28). Its aim was to cut Kenji's network off at its central clinic, and the child's death was a cost its planners accepted. No one in the world identifies the attackers, and no one claims it. Danny Shepard, Hannah's adoptive brother, dies outside the building in the same blast. The oath, the wait, and the 2153 conception stand as written.

**5. The resistance and the conspiracy.** Canon's Cerberus does not exist. Harper's black cell is the **Third Hand**, inside the **Charter Front**. The author-side label "Cerberus" belongs to a separate organization, the conspiracy of ruling 28, which has no link to Harper or the Front.

**11. Who knows what.** Nobody in the story is ever told that Kenji is a transmigrator, and nobody learns where his foresight comes from. The reader learns it from his POV (Overview.md). Hannah learns the enemy's name in 2170 (age 139). Brooks and Alexandra learn it later, as set in the Overview. Until then Kenji gives only his hypothetical: what if what killed the aliens on Mars, or the makers of the alien FTL artifacts, came back hostile? Vance knows he traded his memories for treatment and that the droids' combat routines came from them. He is never told how Kenji knows what he knows, and he does not know about the Reapers. Nobody else is told where the droids' fighting style comes from.

**15. The framing of 2125.** The public record says the delegates were killed by the Russian Federation. No in-universe document contains evidence of the truth. The truth, which POVs may show:
- A few UNIN delegates were verified members of the conspiracy, working with channels inside the Russian Federation. They were partly responsible for the 2059 bombing and the 2065 withdrawals. Brooks found this and reported it. Kenji decided on the operation, and Brooks carried it out. His motive was revenge.
- **The count (decision).** The delegation held fifteen delegates. Fourteen died in the attack. Four of the fourteen were the verified conspirators. The other ten were innocent and died as cover. The fifteenth was absent that day and lived. Kenji carries the ten as a cost he chose.
- Harper's file is right that the Russian Federation was framed for the killing. It cannot see that Russia did conspire against Kenji, and it cannot say by whom. He proves nothing.
- The UNIN does not annul the charters. It decries its helplessness to win justice for its slain, and Kenji takes up the burden of justice for all humankind.

**17. The Protagonist's POV.** Kenji carries POV from A2 on, **written in the first person** (author ruling, 3 November 2026). He carries 7 of A2's 20 micro-chapters (A2.1, A2.4, A2.6, A2.9, A2.12, A2.13, A2.15), none of A3's 10, and 1 of A4's 10. In every other chapter he carries at most 3 or 4 per 10 micro-chapters. No Kenji POV is dated after 2064 until B1.2, where the realization lands. A1 stays as published, with him unnamed and without POV. From the proclamation in 2125 he is also the public Emperor, seen by every POV.

**19. The early pool.** Before 2072 the characters old enough to carry POV are Kenji, Hannah, Brooks, Manswell, Tom and Ruth Shepard, Vance (ruling 30), Teodor Ashby (b. 2030, locked, Author-built), and the three main-cast recruits of ruling 33 (Marchetti, Byron Pyrrus, and Eleanor Whitcombe). The found-family servants and network operatives carry no POV. In chapters A to C, up to two of five micro-chapters per chapter are generic OC slots. Movement 1 (A1 to A3) is exempt from the 20 percent guide, because its OC voices carry the technology showcase (9 of 35). From Movement 2 on, generic OC voices stay under 20 percent (7 of 50). In D to F at most three of the five added slots may be OC, and G keeps one.

**20. Reveal discipline.** POVs may show the truth of the 2065 withdrawals, the 2125 killing, and the conspiracy where the POV character could know it. No in-universe document may contain it, and the conspiracy never names itself on the page before Kenji's network discovers it.

**21. Democracy against empire.** The argument is seen through Hannah's, Brooks's, and Kenji's POVs.

**28. The conspiracy.** Cerberus is an organization that acts against Kenji from 2055, when its first buyer approaches a client's physician. Its method is shadow control of the world's most advanced technologies, his above all, through cutouts, firms, regulators, and licensors. It is called "Cerberus" or "they," never "it." Its moves are the physician's offer (Nov 2055), the theft of five Level 2 Med-Pods (Jun 2057), which it cannot reproduce, three offers to buy Oyama's patents (Jan, Apr, and Oct 2058), the Galveston raid (Nov 2058), the 2059 bomb, and the forged letter that closes Galveston (Oct 2061). The network learns the name at the third offer (see ruling 34). After these the network knows it as one organization but not who leads it, and Brooks is recruited in 2062 to find out. The four delegates of 2125 were partly responsible for the 2059 bomb and the 2065 withdrawals only, so it is larger than they were. It survives 2125. It is author-side in the chapters, and its payoff depends on how the story unfolds.

**29. Patron seats.** Ten patron-built OC seats exist (Overview.md). Their roles are hidden from patrons and kept in an author-only file, never here. Patrons cannot influence Kenji, Hannah, Tom, Ruth, Brooks, or any canon or OC character the author has not opened.

**30. Dominic Vance.** Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance, USMC, retired (name and rank are placeholders), is a locked recurring OC and member of the main cast. Facts his profile lacks:
- In Act I he carries POV in A2.7 (2051 to spring 2059) and A3.1 (2062) only, and his A3.1 must match every gesture of the published A1.5.
- Kenji uses the copy of his memories to create and hone the security droids' combat abilities, and the droids' routines are the only place it lives. This supersedes "knowledge upload never spreads" for this case only.
- He is the first recruit and first member of the secret network (summer 2051) and its first leader. He brings in Ashby (autumn 2051), expands the team twice before Byron is recruited (July 2052), and walks with Kenji until the L5 guards are finished (2055). He scouts Brooks, recruits her, and brings her to Kenji. When Brooks takes over the network he becomes Kenji's personal guard, and later Legion Master of the Custodian Legion.

**31. Security and operatives (author ruling, revised 3 November 2026).** The network begins in summer 2051 with Vance, then Ashby, who guard Kenji. Vance's fighters wear Level 1 powered-suit frames, and the date of Level 2 suits is open. From autumn 2052 the private sites are guarded by Level 2 security droids built by Oyama, run by Level 2 Dumb AI on Kenji's data crystals, and upgraded to Level 3 after the June 2057 theft (decision). Two Level 5 droids are with Kenji at all times from 2055. They look and move like men, and Hannah takes them for men until 2060. Operatives carry no POV, apart from Vance, who is main cast and not an operative in this sense.

**32. Med-Pod tiers and longevity (author ruling).** Only Level 2 Med-Pods and better can rejuvenate and extend longevity. The public Primitive grade cures and mends only. Anyone in this file who lives past a natural span was treated at Level 2 or better. Tom and Ruth Shepard live to 116 because Kenji gives them Level 2 treatment as found family, and not because of the public clinics (decision). Tom is treated in the hidden wing in 2053, because his adopted son Danny asked Kenji to save him. Ruth takes the same treatment after she meets Kenji, and refuses the upload (decision). Where older text says public-grade for a long-lived character, it is superseded by this ruling.

**33. The recruits who become main cast (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Byron Pyrrus (owner of Oyama Industries, a Mass Effect canon corporation), Helen Marchetti (a Houston lawyer, Tallis Bay's trustee), and Eleanor Whitcombe (a senator who leads the organization's political factions) are main cast. Each later becomes one of the Imperium's top administrators (titles open). Kenji recruits Byron in July 2052, which is why Whitcombe is not the first elite buyer. Names, ages, and details are placeholders unless stated. Each has a profile and a POV chapter in A2 (A2.3, A2.2, A2.5), and Whitcombe has a second in A4.3.

**34. How Cerberus is named (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** The network does not learn the name from money trails or documents. It learns it at the third attempt to buy Oyama's technology patents (October 2058), when the other side's lawyer tries to frighten Marchetti with the threat of violence from "Cerberus." The raiders of November 2058 say nothing.

**35. Oyama Industries and the robots (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama builds the Primitive Med-Pods after Byron is recruited (Kenji builds no Primitive pod). It also sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots, Level 1 powered suits, Tokamak reactors, Level 1 anti-gravity, and Gundarium Alpha once orbital infrastructure is complete. Byron is also the first target of Cerberus.

## 5. Relationships and events between characters

### The Shepards and Kenji
- Tom is dying of pancreatic cancer in 2053 and becomes one of Kenji's first recruits, so the Shepards are the first family Kenji grows close to. Tom takes the training upload but not the directive, becomes the clinic facilities chief, and is among the night staff Hannah gets out in 2059.
- Ruth's question is in her entry above.

### Vance, Brooks, and Kenji
- **The recruitment order (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Vance first (summer 2051), then Ashby (autumn 2051), then the lawyers in October 2051 with the two of them standing guard, then a larger team, then Byron in July 2052. Vance expands the team again a few months before Byron.
- **The name Cerberus.** See ruling 34.
- **Brooks's recruitment.** After the October 2061 letter closes Galveston, Vance scouts her from the ministry's own files and Kenji approves (A2.13) and her paper on the Ankara tariff schedules. He recruits her in Marseille in 2062 (A1.5 from her side, A3.1 from his). Her answer to the recruiter's question is to ask who is giving the cure away and what it costs the one who gives.
- **Brooks meets Kenji.** A week later, in a rented office above a ship chandler in Marseille. She counts the exits and asks for a window that opens. The unsolved 2059 file remains the first she is handed.
- Vance approves of her counting and counts nothing himself.

### Brooks and Harper (2112 to 2150)
- From 2112 (age 74) Brooks notices that someone in UNAS counterintelligence is auditing the hidden service's Action work, because his tradecraft does not repeat either. She has no name for him.
- In 2065 she saw the lenders withdraw in a coordinated pattern, with one exception: Kenji, who did not withdraw but increased his stake. She concluded that someone was working against Manswell and possibly against him. She hunted that hand for sixty years, starting from the 2059 file, the first she was handed in 2062 and the one she never closes.
- In early 2125 (age 87) her Collection layer verified that four UNIN delegates were members of the conspiracy and linked to channels in the Russian Federation. She reported it. Kenji alone decided on the operation, and she carried it out, and the framing of the Russian Federation followed. Her conditioning removes betrayal and not disagreement, and she had pressed him to act sooner.
- She watched the same counterintelligence officer build the case against Russia and find it too clean. At the proclamation days later she becomes Grand Marshal of the Inquisition, the UNAS rosters reach her desk, and she names him: Jack Harper. From 2135 she holds his full dossier, face included. They never meet or speak.
- She marks the 2059 file closed in 2125 for the four she found, but keeps the page. The file proves nothing about who else was behind it, and her habit is to keep the page. Her network has known since 2061 that the organization exists but never who leads it, so she believes the account is settled.
- At the Arcturus surrender in 2150 (age 112) she watches Harper walk out through the lower decks with the Emperor's approval. He never sees her. She lets him go because the network he will rebuild will lead her to every cell worth finding, and because the file he carries proves nothing. From then on every cell she arrests and every courier she follows tells her where the next one is.

### Hannah and the two Level 5 guards
- From 2055 two Level 5 droids are with Kenji at all times. They look and move like men. Hannah first sees them in 2056 as the two quiet men who follow Kenji, and she takes them for security. Kenji tells her what they are in 2060 (A2.12).

### The secret between them
The 2125 operation was Kenji's decision, and Brooks carried it out. Harper's file is right that the Russian Federation was framed for the killing, and it is unprovable. He cannot say who did it, and he does not know it was her. He also cannot see why. The first time she hears the accusation on a pirate broadcast, her conditioning will let her lie but will not let her feel nothing. The Wong broadcast is the first time she hears the accusation aloud.

### Hannah and the truth of 2125
- Whether she ever learns the truth about 2125 depends on how events unfold. Neither outcome is ruled out.
- She lost the same child, so her anger would have two targets: the killers and the lie.
