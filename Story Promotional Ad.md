# *Those Who Walked Out Whole* & *The Centuries That Became Days*
## Official Reader's Guide & Q&A

> *“Patients crossed borders to reach the clinics... They are written in a stunned, flat register, like those of survivors of an accident who have not yet decided whether to believe they are alive.”*

---

### **WHAT IS THE STORY ABOUT?**

In March 2050, a man wakes up in a body he doesn't recognize with blueprints in his mind for technology that shouldn't exist. Calling himself Kenji Himura, he builds a crude machine in a dusty Texas tire shop that can heal any injury, cure any disease, and reverse human aging. 

Knowing the world would tear itself apart—or crush him—to own it, Kenji begins recruiting a shadowy inner circle of dying leaders, ex-marines, and ruthless legal minds. In exchange for their lives, they give him their absolute loyalty, building a secret global empire designed to protect the technology at all costs.

Spanning two decades, *Those Who Walked Out Whole* and *The Centuries That Became Days* follow the quiet, dangerous rise of an underground network that scales from hidden coastal clinics to the birth of humanity’s first star-crossing fleet—and the invisible war fought to keep it all from burning down.

### **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (NO SPOILERS)**

#### **Q: What genre does this fall into?**
**A:** It is a high-concept, slow-burn speculative thriller that bridges **hard sci-fi** with **techno-noir and political intrigue**. Think *The Expanse* meets *Station Eleven*—grounded, atmospheric, and deeply human science fiction where miracles come with heavy geopolitical and personal consequences.

#### **Q: Who is Kenji Himura? Is he an alien, a time traveler, or something else?**
**A:** That is the central mystery driving the saga. Kenji remembers nothing of his past, yet he possesses intimate knowledge of physics and engineering decades ahead of human civilization. He isn't a typical hero or a mad scientist—he's a quiet, obsessive architect trying to build an interstellar sanctuary before the world catches up to him.

#### **Q: What is the "A Life for a Life" trade?**
**A:** Kenji doesn't sell his miracle Med-Pods for trillions of dollars. Instead, he target-recruits terminally ill high-level figures—an industrial tycoon, a razor-sharp corporate lawyer, a powerful U.S. Senator, an elite Marine gunnery sergeant—and offers them total physical restoration. The cost? Absolute loyalty, fake deaths, new identities, and an unalterable contract to serve his shadow network for the rest of their long lives.

#### **Q: Who is Hannah Shepard, and why is she so central to the plot?**
**A:** Hannah is a former Navy officer and clinic security chief defined by her ruthless eye for detail. Unlike others who accept Kenji's miracles at face value, Hannah makes it her mission to unravel his secrets. Her years-long game of psychological chess with Kenji evolves into one of the story's most compelling relationships—a marriage built on deep love, mutual respect, and dangerous hidden truths.

#### **Q: What is "Cerberus"?**
**A:** Cerberus is the shadowy, highly funded adversary operating in the background. While Kenji’s network is nearly invincible on the ground, Cerberus attacks through bureaucratic warfare—forged government audits, attempted corporate buyouts, and covert sabotage. They are the constant, unseen threat driving the story's high-stakes intelligence game.

#### **Q: How fast does the technology evolve?**
**A:** The story spans from 2050 to the mid-2060s. It traces an extraordinary compounding leap from a single scrap-built medical bay in Texas to automated disassembler arrays, military security droids, powered exoskeletons, and ultimately humanity's first Faster-Than-Light (FTL) engines capable of crossing light-years in days.

### **WHY READ IT?**

* **Grounded Miracles:** Science fiction that treats world-changing technology not as magic, but as physical infrastructure with real-world rust, logistics, and legal consequences.
* **Complex Moral Gray Areas:** A faction that saves thousands of dying children while enforcing total secrecy, corporate infiltration, and mental failsafes.
* **Sharp, Restrained Prose:** Atmospheric storytelling that favors sensory detail, sharp dialogue, and quiet emotional impact over mindless technobabble.
* **An Epic Narrative Scale:** A story that moves effortlessly from intimate hospital rooms and quiet diners to global corporate rooms and the cold edge of interstellar space.
