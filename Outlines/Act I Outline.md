## 1. Shape of the act

- **What changes.** Humanity is handed cures and the knowledge to deliver them, then ships and machines, by no one it can name. The reader sees who is behind the gift (Kenji), the network he builds so that no one can find him, the pressure that taught him to build it, and what it costs him. The cavern then shows that humanity was not first, and Charon turns out to be a door.
- **Ends on.** The road runs out at the second jump, and in the letters dread overtakes fascination.
- **The engine.** Each chapter answers one question and raises the next: who is giving us this and what does it cost them (A, B), what lies behind the dead, and will it return (C).
- **Tone.** Wonder in A, strain in B, unease in C. The act closes on a line the in-universe record supplies: after the relay, the question "belonged to locksmiths."
- **Link to the Prologue.** A1, A2, and A3 are the Prologue's three doors (the body, the stars, the work of the world). A2 now carries the answer from inside before the world asks the question in A4.
- **Dramatic irony.** From A2 the reader sees what the in-universe history cannot. Each excerpt is the scholar's account, and the micro-chapters complete or contradict it.

## 2. Chapter by chapter

**Key.** Type: [I] investigative, [A] action, [H] hybrid. Ending (End): P physical event, Q quiet reversal, B held breath, W withheld answer, E emotional beat. - **No operatives before the network.** The network is founded at the end of A2.3, with the marine as its first member. Before that, the private sites are guarded by robots alone, and no human operative appears in any chapter dated earlier.
- **The tech chapters.** By 2060 these technologies exist in the setting at the levels shown. Each is shown through someone who touches it, and some chapters carry two or three. **Kenji starts the tech-level system in A2.2**: he writes the grades himself, and the public and private levels below are his own rule for what may leave the room.
- **Robots as site security.** From 2052 the private sites are guarded by Level 2 robot sentries (Level 1 Dumb AI) and, once the network exists, human operatives beside them. From 2053 they are guarded by Level 3 droids, with the operatives in Level 2 powered suits. Public Level 1 robots are fixed arms and simple mobile units under human control, and no public robot guards anything.
- **From 2061, yards and warp.** A Kenji, Brooks, Hannah, or Tom POV shows the primitive starship assembly yards and then the first tests of Primitive warp.
- **The foundation order (author ruling, fourth revision).** Kenji sees that selling his inventions at once would make many enemies, so he builds a safety net first. (1) A Level 2 Med-Pod that restores a dying person to full health and also rejuvenates and extends life (A2.2). (2) Veterans and experts in combat and other fields, who serve a set number of years in exchange for being restored to their prime, and who become the secret organization that protects him (A2.3, A2.4). (3) When it is ready, the same treatment for the world's elites, to win favor and build influence so he can build his base without much trouble (from 2053, A2.4). (4) After the wedding in 2058, he slowly and secretly spreads his inventions through legal and shell companies. The elites are his main income, and their fees fund the public Primitive clinics.
- **Danny Shepard (author ruling, fourth revision).** Tom's adopted son and Hannah's adoptive brother, a former Army sapper and one of the marine's first recruits. In 2053 he asks whether the treatment can save his dying adoptive father, and that is how Kenji meets the Shepards (A2.5). He cannot tell his family what he does. He dies outside the building in the 2059 bomb (A1.4), on overwatch in the car park outside Hannah's roster. He carries no POV. His profile is Daniel_Shepard_Character_Profile.md.
- **Security from 2053 (author ruling, fourth revision).** The network's operatives wear Level 2 powered suits, and the private sites are guarded by Level 3 droids on a data crystal system that nobody at the time can hack. Elite clients who know of him will not risk their long life by fighting him, because their follow-up treatments come only through him. Cerberus is the exception. His members are human and his droids are not sentient, so they make mistakes, and a dedicated enemy can still reach him and the people he loves.
- **Cerberus is uncovered (author ruling, fourth revision).** The break-in (A2.3), the shakedown (A2.6), the bomb (A1.4), and the theft of five Level 2 Med-Pods (A2.4, 2061) are laid side by side by the network, which finds one organization, its method, and its cutouts, but not who leads it. Brooks is recruited in 2062 to find out. Cerberus cannot reproduce the pods, because it lacks the knowledge and expertise in Med-Pod and data crystal technology. Once his inventions spread and it traces them to him, the two are at secret war. The author-side label is "Cerberus," and the network's own working name for it is open.

*A2a: the build (a fixed past)*
1. A2.1 Kenji (30), 2050 [I] · Q. He wakes in a body that is not his, in a flat that is. His hands finish a diagram he does not remember starting. He suspects what has happened and says nothing, since he has no one to say it to. No Reaper thought and no thought that this was once a story he read. His wanting-to-be-wanted flinch shows once. Hook: the diagram has a name at the top, in his own handwriting, that he cannot read yet.
2. A2.2 Kenji (31), 2051 [I] · B. **The Med-Pod, the tiers, and the safety net.**
   - Tools first, then a full Level 2 Med-Pod benched in private. It works. It restores a dying person to full health, and it rejuvenates and extends life. Then he reads what it is: a machine that rebuilds a body would make anyone who owned it a target for every government, hospital chain, and criminal who heard of it. He sees that selling anything he builds now would make a great many enemies at once, and that he would be fighting uphill.
   - **He decides on a safety net first (author ruling).** Before he releases anything, he will gather veterans and experts in combat and other fields who will serve him for a set number of years in exchange for being restored to their prime, and they will become a secret organization that protects him. When it is ready, he will offer the same treatment to the world's elites, who will pay heavily, and the favor and influence they owe him will let him build his base without much trouble.
   - He builds a cruder unit beside it, a Primitive model that cures but does not renew, so that something can safely be shown to the public later. He decides that only the Primitive grade will ever leave the room.
   - **He starts the tier system here.** He writes the grades on a sheet and pins it above the bench: Primitive for what may be released, a higher level for what stays behind. Every later technology gets the same two columns. He notes that the Primitive grade must always be one the world could have invented unaided, or the question of who built it will have an answer.
   - The lawyer who files his first shell company asks who owns the design, and he gives a name he invented that morning. The lawyer writes it down without looking up.
   - He notes that the L2 unit, and whatever it is worth, needs a guard he cannot hire. He starts a Level 2 robot in the corner of the shed.
   - Hook: the pen is over the filing for the shell, and the L2 unit is under a tarp behind him. The chapter cuts before he signs. *Tech: Med-Pod.*
3. A2.3 Kenji (32), 2052 [H] · B. **The robots, the first break-in, and the decision to build a network.**
   - A rented warehouse east of Houston. There is no staff, no operative, and no guard, only three Level 2 sentry robots, each run by a Level 1 Dumb AI task controller, which he built over the winter in the shed and trained on the marine's memories. He sits in the monitor room with a cold coffee and the L2 Med-Pod under its tarp on the floor below. Slow Gear: the smell of solder and wet concrete, and what each machine is for.
   - A crew of four breaks in for the machine. They were hired by a broker none of them has met, and one says it is "worth a hospital." Nobody knows who sent them. It is the first of the incidents the network later lays side by side.
   - Fast Gear: the robots disarm and restrain the crew without killing anyone. One sentry takes a gun from a crewman's hand. Another pushes a crewman face down onto the floor and binds his wrists. The crew never reaches the tarp. Kenji squares the stack of monitors on the desk. The text names no feeling.
   - He calls an anonymous tip to the sheriff, the robots go dark in their crates, and the crew is picked up at the loading dock. The building shows nothing.
   - Afterward he washes his hands at the utility sink. He is quietly pleased with himself for having prepared. He is also grateful for something else: the sentries fought as well as they did because their combat routines were built from the memories, experience, and expertise of a retired Marine who bartered them for treatment the autumn before (told in one passage, off-page: the dying sergeant in a hospice who had nothing to pay with but what was in his head, and the two-way upload that copied his memories out and gave him his lungs back). Kenji used that copy to create the droids' combat abilities and then to hone them, running the sentries through the sergeant's engagements until they moved as he did. The sentry that takes a gun from a crewman and pins him to the floor uses the same movements the sergeant used in the engagements Kenji copied, and Kenji recognizes them from the sergeant's memories.
   - He concludes that the robots stopped the four men only after they entered the building. No one had warned him of the four men beforehand, and no one is watching for threats to his people. He decides to build the network that the safety net calls for, starting with the marine.
   - Hook: he picks up the phone and dials the marine. He has met the man only twice, at the treatment and at the upload. He does not trust him from acquaintance. He chooses him for two reasons: the marine is the only person who knows what the machine does and has told no one in the months since, and Kenji has read his memories closely while building the droids' routines, so he knows how the marine acted under fire and how he treated the men under his command. The chapter cuts before the call is answered. *Tech: Robotics L2, Dumb AI L1 (first seen).*
4. A2.4 Marine (Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance, placeholder), 2052 to 2061 [I] · Q. **The network grows.** (Locked recurring OC, main cast. First leader of the network, scout and recruiter of Brooks, later Kenji's personal guard and Legion Master of the Custodian Legion. The chapter spans nine years and is ordered by its start date. It is told as the marine's own ledger of dated entries: 2052, 2053, 2054, 2056, 2058, 2059, 2061.)
   - His habit of attention is what can be defended and what cannot. He reads ground, cover, sight lines, and distance with his body, by where he would stand, and not by counts. He counts nothing, so he does not clash with Brooks.
   - Growth: the safety net, in his own ledger. He finds veterans and experts in combat and other fields, each serving a set number of years for being restored to their prime. Shell companies in several countries, the clinics that carry them, dying professionals hired as managers, and a few discarded specialists (a retired analyst, a fixer, a locksmith) who owe their lives to the machine. His want is the next site secured before the rollout date.
   - **2052.** He recruits Danny Shepard, a former Army sapper who lost the use of his legs under a collapsed building in 2047, and who serves a seven-year term for his treatment.
   - **2053.** The elite rollout begins, with the Level 2 powered suits and the Level 3 droids on the data crystal system. The Galveston clinic opens in June as the public face, and its hidden wing treats recruits and elites. Danny asks whether the treatment can save his dying adoptive father. The marine takes it to Kenji, and Kenji says yes.
   - **2054.** The first incident of the elite years: a client's household broker is approached by a stranger asking what the treatment is. The network turns the man away without learning who sent him.
   - **2056.** The droids stand in the hidden wing's service corridor beside the human guards, in time for the shakedown. Tom tells the staff they are equipment.
   - **2058.** The wedding, which he watches from the back door.
   - **2059.** The bomb. Danny is on overwatch in the car park, outside Hannah's roster. He sees the delivery van riding low, goes after it, and dies near the blast. The marine writes the entry without adjectives.
   - **2061.** Five Level 2 Med-Pods are stolen from a site outside Tulsa in one night, with every door still locked and the droids' logs clean. The network lays the break-in, the shakedown, the bomb, and the theft side by side. They are one organization. It knows his work, it uses paper, cutouts, and money, and it has no names the network can find.
   - Closing failure: he concludes that guards and locked doors are not enough, and that he lacks the skill to find out who leads them.
   - Hook: he starts looking for someone who can do what he cannot, and he opens a file on Brooks. In 2061 she is a ministry analyst in Marseille, dying. *Tech: Robotics L3 (droids with operatives in Level 2 suits), Med-Pod L2 wing, Data Crystal.*

6. A2.6 Hannah (23 and 25), Galveston, 2054 and 2056 [I] · P. **The first answer, the first no, and the shakedown.** About 850 words, at the top of the hybrid range because it holds three scenes.
   - **Opening detail.** The man across the table finishes everything on his plate, including the parsley. She wants to know who eats like that.
   - **Scene 1, November 2054, her parents' kitchen (Slow Gear).** Sunday dinner. She is a Navy lieutenant home on leave, and this is the first time she has seen Kenji. Her father is cheerful and her mother is watching everyone. Her mother has set a fourth place for Danny, who is in Houston on contract and has not been home in a year. Kenji looks at the empty chair for half a second too long. She refused his upload and is polite and cold. She asks what he does when he is not at the clinic, and he says, "I build things." She asks what things, and he says, "Tools, mostly." It is a true answer and a small one. He clears the plates without being asked and squares the stack. She leaves not liking him, but he has not lied to her.
   - **Scene 2, March 2056, the clinic (Slow Gear).** She left the Navy this spring, and her father talked her into the security post. Three weeks in, she walks the building with Kenji and lists what is missing: the license, the name tags, the locked wing, two grey frames the size of a man standing in the back corridor (her father calls them "equipment"), a door crew better trained than a clinic would pay for. He says yes or no to each one. She asks what the grey frames are, and he says, "I can't answer that." He says yes when she asks to retrain the door crew her way, and it is done by Friday. To every other question he gives the same answer, to her face: "I can't answer that." She says, "Which of these can you explain?" He says, "None of them, to you, yet." She says, "Yet." That evening she writes the first line on her list: why he stands at the edge of every room.
   - **Scene 3, a Tuesday in June 2056, the back corridor (Fast Gear).** Men from a hospital chain arrive with a letter and a tool bag. Her father is in the corridor fixing a valve. Two operatives she did not hire stand there and will not look at her. The two grey frames stand beside the wing door. Hannah steps between the men and the wing door and tells them to leave. At the far end Kenji stands with one hand on the door frame, doing nothing. She takes it for fear. Her fist locks around her father's tape measure. A man takes a pipe from the bag.
   - **Hook (P).** Her father's hand goes to his back pocket. Kenji's hand stays on the door frame. The pipe comes up, servos shriek behind her, and a metal hand closes around the hand that holds the pipe. A second metal hand catches the pipe. The men are on the floor and tied, and two robots stand over them. The droids are Level 3, smoother than the older sentries and still loud, and they cross the corridor before Hannah has finished turning. She never sees them start, and learns what they are only when the men are down. The hospital chain's letter is the second of the incidents the network later lays side by side.
   - **Voice.** She notices missing people and missing answers. *Tech: Robotics L3 (droids), Med-Pod L2 (wing).*
7. A2.7 Kenji (37), Galveston, 2057 [I] · Q. **The kidney and the apology.** About 900 words.
   - **Opening detail.** Dr. Okonkwo sets a cooler on the front desk, and Hannah measures the lid with a tape before she will sign for it.
   - **Scene 1, an evening in April 2057 (Slow Gear).** Okonkwo is a transplant surgeon. Her patient needs a cloned kidney before midnight, when the registry gives his slot to someone else. Her waiting lists used to run to pages, and the clinics have emptied them. Kenji thinks about what the kidney is for. It is for one patient tonight. After tonight every hospital in the region will want one, and the pods he can build in a year will fill a county's list and not a state's. He has promised himself that the spread waits until the network is ready, and last June's shakedown showed him what waiting is for. His worry is for the people in the building: Hannah at the desk and Tom in the corridor stand in front of whoever comes for it. Hannah is on shift. She writes the lid's length on the custody sheet and asks where the donor line is. He says, "There is no donor." She goes quiet and asks nothing more. He is relieved, and he dislikes that he is. Okonkwo reads the tag on the cooler and finds the clinic's licensed name, a batch number, the state's approval stamp, and no maker. The clinic is licensed through a shell, with the license in a drawer (A2.5). She tucks the tag into her coat and does not ask, because the paperwork is in order and the kidney works. Kenji sees that nobody asks who made a thing when they need it. His small want is the custody sheet signed and the cooler out the door before midnight.
   - **Scene 2, 12:40 a.m., the staff kitchen (Slow Gear).** The cooler is gone and the patient will live. Hannah puts a plate of cold eggs in front of him, and he finishes it. She says, "You finish everything." He says, "I didn't always have enough." (This is his one sentence of past life.) Then she asks about last summer. He says the two robots are the clinic's guards, and they moved the moment the pipe came up, which is what they are for. He stood still because there was nothing for him to do. She asks where they came from, and he says, "I can't answer that." What he is sorry for is that he never told her they were there. She is the head of security, and he let her think they were equipment. She says, "I thought you were afraid." He says, "I wasn't afraid of the men. I was watching to see that you were clear of them."
   - **Hook (Q).** He had thought she took the job for her father's sake. She tells him she took it to find out what he was hiding, and that she is staying because he finally told her something true about the building. Then she asks if there is more coffee. He understands that he would now least like to lose the one person who is looking at him closely.
   - **Voice.** Kenji asks what a thing is for. *Tech: Cloning L1, Med-Pod L1.*
8. A2.8 Hannah (26), Galveston port and a restaurant, autumn 2057 [I] · E. **The weight and the first dinner.** About 900 words.
