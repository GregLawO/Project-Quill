# Act I Outline: The Gift

Companion to Story_Structure.md (Section 12A), Excerpt_Bank.md, and the Elastic Pace System (Revised). Act I covers Chapters A to C, 2050 to 2075, in 120 micro-chapters as currently cast. **Revised 3 November 2026 (author ruling):** the backstory of A2 is rebuilt around the safety-net plan. Kenji builds protectors before he sells anything, including a producer (Byron Pyrrus of Oyama Industries), a political arm (Eleanor Whitcombe), and a legal team (Helen Marchetti). He sells health and youth to the world's elites from 2055, meets the Shepards through Danny in 2053, and is found by Cerberus, which starts a secret war. The wedding and Brooks's recruitment now sit in A2c, and A2a and A2b build the foundation and introduce the new main cast. The realization comes in three waves. Only A1 (5 micro-chapters) is published. A2 and A3 are open. B1 to B4 are cast at 10 each, released as two batches of 5. C1 to C7 keep their original casts of 5, and C1 to C5 and C7 are extended to 10 each when drafted. C6 stays at 5. Movement names, endings, batch lines, echoes, wants, and re-cuts are proposals (decision).

## 1. Shape of the act

- **What changes.** Humanity is handed cures and the knowledge to deliver them, then ships and machines, by no one it can name. The reader sees who is behind the gift (Kenji), the protectors he builds before he sells anything, the enemy that finds him anyway, and what it costs him. The cavern then shows that humanity was not first, and Charon turns out to be a door.
- **Ends on.** The road runs out at the second jump, and in the letters dread overtakes fascination.
- **The engine.** Each chapter answers one question and raises the next: who is giving us this and what does it cost them (A, B), what lies behind the dead, and will it return (C).
- **Tone.** Wonder in A, strain in B, unease in C. The act closes on a line the in-universe record supplies: after the relay, the question "belonged to locksmiths."
- **Link to the Prologue.** A1, A2, and A3 are the Prologue's three doors (the body, the stars, the work of the world).
- **Dramatic irony.** From A2 the reader sees what the in-universe history cannot. Each excerpt is the scholar's account, and the micro-chapters complete or contradict it.
- **The secret war.** The network learns the enemy's name in October 2058, from Cerberus's own lawyer, and the reader learns it with them. The public never does. Kenji's enemy is known by name, but its leaders and its size are not.

## 2. Chapter by chapter

**Key.** Type: [I] investigative, [A] action, [H] hybrid. Ending (End): P physical event, Q quiet reversal, B held breath, W withheld answer, E emotional beat. Batch line: what a batch of five ends on emotionally. Echo: the earlier element a batch returns to. Wants: the small want and deadline given to each exposition narrator.

### Movement 1: The Three Doors

A1 (the body) is live and unchanged. Everything below it is open.

**The A2 plan in plain terms (author ruling, 3 November 2026 revision).**

1. **The problem (2051).** Kenji sees that selling his inventions will make him powerful enemies, and that every sale is a fight he would lose alone. Before he sells anything, he builds a safety net.
2. **The Level 2 Med-Pod (2051).** It heals a dying person fully. It also rejuvenates, and it slows aging for a lifetime. Only Level 2 does any of this. The cruder Primitive grade he builds beside it cures disease and injury and nothing more.
3. **The protectors (summer 2051 to 2055).** He finds veterans and experts who will serve him for seven years, renewable by choice, in exchange for being restored to health or to their prime. Before they sign he tells them the upload carries a directive that keeps them from telling outsiders what they do (decision). The seven-year term replaces the older ten-year term, to match Danny's profile. Dates move from the plan's 2050 to 2051, because the Med-Pod does not exist before 2051 (decision). **The order of recruitment is an author ruling: Vance, then Ashby, then the lawyers, then a larger team, then Byron.**
   - **a. Vance (summer 2051).** Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance, a dying Marine, is the first recruit. He trades his memories, experience, and expertise for treatment, and Kenji copies them out. He becomes the leader of Kenji's guards.
   - **b. Ashby (autumn 2051).** Vance brings in Teodor Ashby, a radio technician from his old squad. Vance and Ashby are Kenji's security detail from this point on.
   - **c. Lawyers (October 2051).** Two partners of a medium-sized Houston firm: Helen Marchetti, with failing kidneys, and Samuel Ortega, with heart disease (placeholder names (decision)). Vance and Ashby stand guard while Kenji recruits them. They become his legal team and set up Tallis Bay. **Marchetti is main cast and later a top Imperium administrator. She needs a profile.**
   - **d. The team grows (winter 2051 to spring 2052).** Vance expands the team twice. In the winter he adds a first squad of four fighters. In the spring, a few months before Byron, he adds four more fighters and three intelligence staff. The intelligence team (two former intelligence officers and a hacker) finds Kenji's buyers first, then his other targets. A platoon of forty fighters is in place by autumn 2053. Danny Shepard, an Army sapper with legs crushed in 2047, joins in autumn 2052. **The fighters wear Level 1 powered-suit frames (the unpowered exoskeleton).** Kenji is swamped with work and cannot pay for materials, much less for the mini-reactor that powers the Level 2 suit. Level 2 suits wait for money from the elite sales, and the date is open (decision).
   - **e. The producer (July 2052).** **Byron Pyrrus, the dying owner and chairman of Oyama Industries** (a Mass Effect canon corporation; his age and home town are placeholders (decision)). Oyama becomes the factory that builds what Kenji designs. After Byron is recruited, Oyama makes the Primitive Med-Pods, and Kenji builds no Primitive pod himself (author ruling). Oyama also makes Level 1 to Level 3 robots, Level 1 powered suits, Tokamak fusion reactors, Level 1 (Primitive) anti-gravity, and Gundarium Alpha once the orbital infrastructure is complete. **Byron is main cast and later a top Imperium administrator. He needs a profile.**
   - **f. Communications (autumn 2052).** One more network engineer joins Ashby (decision).
   - **g. Droids.** Oyama sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots. The organization's own security droids are Level 2 at first and are upgraded to Level 3 after the June 2057 theft (decision on the date). Kenji gives Oyama the security-droid design for its internal use and still supplies the data crystals and the Dumb AI. **Two Level 5 droids are with Kenji at all times.** They look and move like men, and people take them for human. He builds them himself, with money from the producer, starting in 2052 and finishing in 2055.
   - **h. The political arm (early 2053).** **Senator Eleanor Whitcombe** (60, placeholder name (decision)), who is hiding a failing heart. She leads the organization's political factions worldwide. She is not the first elite buyer. Kenji has already recruited the chairman, and she joins as a recruit, on the same seven-year terms. **She is main cast and later a top Imperium administrator. She needs a profile.**
4. **The fronts (summer 2053).** Three clinics, through a charity foundation called Tallis Bay: **Galveston, Marseille, and Singapore** (decision). The public floors run the Primitive pods that Oyama makes, and patients are asked to keep the true process secret. The hidden wings run Level 2, for recruits and later for paying elites. **The fronts also offer cloned organs for sale, pre-approved through the network's political faction.** That is why their paperwork is real.
5. **The elites (from spring 2055).** When the protectors are ready, he sells health and youth to people who will pay a great deal for them, through Whitcombe's factions, and he uses the sales to buy favor and influence. Politicians and public figures get health and a long life but not youth, because an old politician who suddenly looks thirty would draw questions. Rich private clients who can step out of public life get full rejuvenation (decision). Every buyer is made to understand, without a word said, that he holds the bigger stick: his men, his guards, and the loss of the treatment if they double-cross him.
6. **The secret war (2055 to 2061).** The elite sales and the pods are how Cerberus hears of him. Cerberus is called by its name or "they," never "it."
   - **November 2055.** A client's physician asks a network nurse how the treatment works and offers money. The nurse reports it.
   - **June 2057.** Cerberus steals five Level 2 pods from a truck, using a forged transfer order. They cannot copy them. They lack the knowledge behind the Level 2 machine and the data crystal, so they can only barely build a crude Primitive version. After the theft Kenji upgrades the security droids from Level 2 to Level 3 (decision).
   - **January 2058.** Oyama's Primitive pod line goes on sale under Oyama's name (decision). **Oyama is Cerberus's first target, before Kenji.** Someone makes a first offer to buy Oyama's technology patents. Byron refuses and tells Kenji.
   - **April 2058.** A second offer arrives, with a forged safety audit behind it. Byron refuses again.
   - **October 2058.** **The third offer is where the network learns the name.** The other side's legal representative, sitting across from Marchetti, tries to frighten her into signing, and says that people who refuse a third time meet "Cerberus." Cerberus is not a name they learn from money trails or documents (author ruling).
   - **November 2058.** Three men raid the Galveston wing at night. The droids stop them. The men say nothing. Nobody in a shadow organization gives up its name that easily.
   - **Spring 2059.** The Galveston bomb.
   - **October 2061.** A forged state letter closes the Galveston clinic. **Only Galveston closes.** Whitcombe's political faction stops the other clinics from closing and forces Galveston to reopen.
7. **The Shepards (2053).** One of his men, Danny Shepard, asks him to save Danny's dying adoptive father, Tom. This is how Kenji meets the Shepards.
8. **Time passes (2053 to 2058).** The organization grows. He gets to know Hannah, they marry in September 2058, and he spreads his inventions through Oyama and through legal and shell companies.
9. **Good protection is not complete safety.** His people are human and make mistakes. A dedicated enemy who is willing to take losses can still reach him and the people he loves.

*A2a: the safety net (2051 to early 2053). Every chapter builds the foundation and introduces a new main-cast member.*

1. **A2.1 Kenji (31), 2051 [I] · B. The Med-Pod, the problem, and the plan.** About 1,000 words, first person. *This merges the old A2.1 and A2.2 into one POV. The drafted A2.1 is replaced, and its wake-up beats are reused. He builds no Primitive pod.*
   - **Opening detail.** The bench in the rented tire shop, with a finished Level 2 Med-Pod on it and the word at the head of an old diagram that he could not read a year ago.
   - **The memory (Slow Gear, short).** He is already building the pod. While he works he remembers the day he woke in a body that was not his, in a flat that was, with his hands finishing a diagram he did not remember starting. He suspected what had happened and told no one. The word he could not read then is the pod's name now. No Reaper thought and no thought that this was once a story. His flinch at being wanted shows once.
   - **The test.** A dog with a broken hip walks out of the pod. He asks what the machine is for. It rebuilds a body, so whoever owns it becomes a target for every government, hospital chain, and criminal who hears of it. He sees that every sale from now on will make him an enemy, and that he would lose the first fight alone.
   - **The plan.** He writes it on a sheet and pins it above the bench. Step one: protectors, starting with a man who knows how to guard. Step two: a factory and buyers with enough power to protect him in public. Step three: everyone else. On the same sheet he starts the tier system in two columns. Primitive is what may leave the room. Level 2 is what stays behind. The Primitive grade must always be something the world could have invented unaided, or someone will ask who built it. He will not build the Primitive grade himself. He needs a factory owner for that, and nothing is sold yet.
   - **The terms.** Seven years of work, renewable only if the person chooses it, with follow-up treatment for as long as they serve.
   - **Hook (B).** Summer 2051. He stands outside the hospice room of a dying Marine named Dominic Vance, who he has decided to ask to build and train his guards, and opens the door. *Tech: Med-Pod L2.*

2. **A2.2 Helen Marchetti (52), 2051 [I] · Q. The lawyers and Tallis Bay.** About 800 words. (New main cast, placeholder name (decision). Habit of attention: who signs, and who can be sued.)

A man she has never met offers both of them a cure for seven years of work. Two men stand at the door: a gray-haired man who looks like a Marine, and a younger one with a radio case. They are his security detail. He tells them what the upload does before they sign. She asks who owns the design. He gives a name he invented that morning, "Tallis Bay," and she writes it down without looking up.
   - **Scene 2, December 2051, the firm after hours (Slow Gear).** Both are well. Want: the foundation filed before the quarter closes, so the first gift can be accepted tax-free. She drafts the seven-year contract and the clause that keeps follow-up treatment going for as long as someone serves.
   - **Hook (Q).** She asks what happens to everyone's term if he dies. He names her the foundation's trustee, which means she can stop him. She understands that she now holds his safety net. *Tech: Med-Pod L2.*

3. **A2.3 Byron Pyrrus (68), July 2052 [I] · Q. The producer.** About 800 words. (New main cast, placeholder age (decision). Habit of attention: what a line can build by Friday, and what it cannot.)

Kenji also hands Byron the security-droid design for Oyama's internal use, with a rule: not for sale. Kenji will supply the data crystals and the Dumb AI himself. Byron asks why. 4. **A2.4 Kenji (32), October 2052 [H] · Q. The protectors.** About 1,000 words, two dated scenes.
   - **Scene 1, a rented warehouse east of Houston (Slow Gear).** Fifteen people are on seven-year terms: the two lawyers, Vance and seven fighters, three intelligence staff, and Ashby and one more communications engineer. The newest fighter is Danny Shepard, restored in September, a sapper who stands with his palm flat on a roof post. The fighters wear Level 1 frames from Oyama. Kenji cannot pay for more, and the Level 2 suit needs a mini-reactor he cannot afford. One squad of four Level 2 security droids, built by Oyama to his design and running his data crystals, stands by the wall. They are loud and they clank. Kenji asks what each machine is for.
   - **Told in one passage.** The hospice. Vance, dying, had nothing to pay with but what was in his head. Kenji copied his memories, experience, and expertise out and gave him his lungs back. The droids' fighting routines come from that copy. When a droid pins a training dummy, Kenji recognizes the way Vance would do it.
   - **Scene 2, a back bench.** Two frames lie half built. They will be his personal guards and will look like men. He is building them himself with money from Oyama, and they will take three years. Until they are done, Vance and Ashby walk with him.
   - **Hook (Q).** He writes 2055 in his notebook. He will sell nothing to anyone until the platoon is full, the droid squads are in place, and every site has a guard. *Tech: MIPS L1, Robotics L2, L5 (in progress), Dumb AI L2, Data Crystal.*

5. **A2.5 Eleanor Whitcombe (60), February 2053 [I] · Q. The political arm.** About 800 words. (New main cast, placeholder name and age (decision). Habit of attention: she watches who looks at whom before they speak.)

Tom took him in at eight, after his own father died when a gantry cable failed. Danny says he has never asked for anything. She found the clinic by an address on a napkin from an old yard foreman, which is how Tom ended up on the public list. Kenji took the license off the wall because a license names an owner. He sees her notice the bare wall and every missing name tag. Danny stands behind him and watches his father walk out whole. Danny puts his flat palm on the wall, then leaves by the side door before Hannah can see him.
   - He writes a blank card and slides it under the door at the moment Tom walks out (the end of A1.1). He wants one person outside the network to know that someone is behind this place, even if he cannot give his name.
   - **Ten days later.** Tom comes back with Ruth to thank someone. Tom took the training upload but not the directive. Tom says Danny is his boy and asks if this has anything to do with him. Kenji says Danny works for a company he runs, and asks them not to tell Hannah. Ruth asks "who are you?" before anyone else ever does. His answer is true and tells her nothing. Tom says "son," and Kenji flinches.

Ashby finds that the alarm stayed silent when it should have rung. He blames himself. Kenji upgrades the security droids to Level 3 that autumn.
