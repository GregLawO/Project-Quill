The micro-chapters carry what the scholar cannot see, so each excerpt can be read as incomplete or wrong. (decision: the "sanctioned information" wording in the Excerpt Bank and Prologue needs to change.)
- **Reaper secrecy (decision, from your last message).** The reader knows. From 2065, Kenji and those he has informed may name the Reapers on the page, in thought and in dialogues with each other. Everyone else, including every OC, knows nothing.
- **Fate (author ruling).** Kenji thinks and feels as if the universe is working against him. The text mostly allows chance or human causes. The Author is Fate, and the beats below are the Author's to sharpen.
- **Reordering (author ruling: options 1 and 3 mixed).** Excerpts keep their order (A4, B1, B2, B3, B4). Micro-chapters are reordered inside each chapter by date. Movement 2 opens with his 2050 to 2064 life, reaches the 2065 realization in B1, then runs forward.
- **Length (author ruling).** From Movement 2 on, every chapter has 10 micro-chapters. Movement 1 stays at five. How a chapter is released (two batches, or one double batch) is decided later.

## 2. Kenji's arc in this movement

**Phase 1, the quiet life (2050 to 2064).** He wakes with knowledge he can't explain, does not know the world is Mass Effect, and chooses patience. He wants a normal life with Hannah. The clinics and the first servants (his found family, 2053 to 2058) are meant to be safe and small. But every gift draws negative interest: probes, press queries, the 2059 bomb that costs the child, the oath of no heir. Each tragedy pushes him from a quiet life toward a defended one, and he builds the hidden service (2058 to 2062) to protect it. He still does not know what he is protecting it from.

**The turn (2065).** Manswell announces the Alpha Centauri expedition and the "familiar organizations" snap into place. This is Mass Effect, and the Reapers are coming. He is terrified, vindicated, and obsessive.

**Phase 2, the uphill fight (2065 to 2069).** Each lever he pulls works, but at a cost, and something always arrives a beat early or late. Fate beats, each with a plausible chance or human cause:

The end state is settled (the Imperium wins by 2150, the Reapers arrive in 2183), so Fate's pressure has to cost him materials, money, time, people, and trust, not the victory.

## 3. Rules for Kenji's chapters (Author rulings)

- **Before 2065 he does not know it is Mass Effect.** No Reaper or canon thoughts. His Slow Gear carries the quiet-life wish, the knowledge he can't account for, and his unease.
- **From 2065 the Reapers may sit in his thoughts.** How he knows still stays hidden from every character, including Hannah and Brooks. Nobody will know he is a transmigrator. He will use the dead aliens in Mars, and later the unknown alien artifacts found all over human space, as justification for his obssessive build-up and plan to unite humanity under his rule.
- **In-universe documents can now name him.** His name now appears in some documents, but only for minor things, his secret network still covers his tracks. The truth of the 2125 is that he managed to track some of those responsible for the bombing that killed his child and uses them as a stepping stone to the Throne.
- **Elastic Pace applies.** Interior in the Slow Gear, the body in the Fast Gear. Brooks still counts, Hannah still notices what is missing, Manswell still reads money, and Kenji reads what a thing is for.
- **OC slots shrink.** With 10 per chapter and more named POVs, generic OC voices should drop below 20 percent of Movement 2.
- **Found family.** Name 3 to 4 of the first dozen servants as recurring voices, each with a clear want.
