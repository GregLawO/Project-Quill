## 12. Story Structure

**Labels (author ruling).** Parts are the seven groups, A to G. Chapters are A1, A2, A3, and so on (32 in all). Micro-chapters are the single-POV pieces inside a chapter, coded A1.1, A1.2, and so on. "Excerpt" means only the quoted epigraph text that opens a chapter.

- **Length (author ruling).** There is no fixed word count. The story is written until it is finished, however long that takes.
- **Order.** The story is written chapter by chapter, starting with the Prologue. The Prologue is a brief in-universe history in the manner of the opening of *The Fellowship of the Ring*: impersonal, demonstrative, and non-technical, with no sign of who wrote it. It is the file Prologue_The_Road_to_the_Stars.md. Act I is planned in detail in Act_I_Outline.md, which governs where an older file disagrees with it.
- **Published canon (author ruling, revised 1 November 2026).** Only A1 is published and canon. A2 and A3 go live on 2 November 2026 and were reopened and revised, so they are not canon yet. The first release is the first batch of A2 (A2.1 to A2.5).
- **The 2160 first phase.** A separate part of the novel, focused on 2160, with mostly alien POVs reacting to the Imperium. The background history reaches the reader through the aliens' questions or through the chapters below. (open) Whether the three acts run as one block before the first phase or interleave with it. Nothing about the first phase is settled until the author chooses.
- **Chapters and micro-chapters (author ruling, revised).** Each chapter is built on an excerpt and made of single-POV micro-chapters from varied walks of life. A1 is published at 5 micro-chapters. A2 has 20, released as four batches of 5, and A3 has 10, released as two. From A4 on every chapter has 10 micro-chapters, released 5 at a time, ordered by date inside the chapter, except C6, which stays at 5. A4 to B4 are cast at 10. C1 to G5 carry their original casts (5 each, D to F carry 7 to 9) and are extended to 10 each when drafted, apart from C6. The casts are in Excerpt_Bank.md, and the Act I casts as revised are in Act_I_Outline.md. Two deliberate date-order exceptions: A3.1 (2062) leads A3 as a step back to the published A1.5, and A2.4 is told as a ledger from 2052 to 2061 and is ordered by its start date.
- **Release plan.** Chapters release weekly in batches of five. With A2 at 20 micro-chapters, A3 at 10, and A4 at 10, Act I is 120 micro-chapters and about 24 weekly batches, replacing the earlier 75 chapters and 15 weeks.
- **Kenji's POV (author ruling, revised).** Kenji Himura carries POV from A2 on. He carries 7 of A2's 20 micro-chapters, none of A3's 10, and 1 of A4's 10, and at most 3 or 4 per 10 micro-chapters in every other chapter. No Kenji POV is dated after 2064 until B1.2, where the realization lands. The excerpts are read from A2 on as an uninformed independent scholar's account that the micro-chapters can contradict. Rules are in Overview.md.
- **The marine (author ruling).** Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance (placeholder name and rank) is a locked recurring OC and member of the main cast. He is the first member and first leader of the secret network, scouts and recruits Brooks, and later guards Kenji and becomes Legion Master of the Custodian Legion. He carries POV in A2.4 and A3.1 in Act I. Rules are in Overview.md.
- **Patron seats (author ruling).** Ten patron-built OC seats appear across Movement 2 and later. Their roles are secret from patrons and kept in an author-only file. Patrons cannot influence Kenji, Hannah, her parents, Brooks, the marine, or any character the author has not opened.
- **Draft format (author ruling).** Each chapter draft opens with its excerpt at the very top: the chapter's title only, the text in italics inside quotation marks, and the attribution "— *The Road to the Stars*". Each micro-chapter's byline (act, movement, code, narrator, place, year) and prose follow. The full rule is in Overview.md. An excerpt's description of how the accounts read applies to the in-universe record only, and the micro-chapters carry full emotion.
- **Style (author ruling).** The Elastic Pace System, revised for serialization, governs every chapter. Endings rotate through five types with no more than two physical events per batch of five, and the strongest hook sits in the fifth chapter of a batch.
- **D to F expansion.** Chapters_D_to_F_Expansion_and_Recurring_OCs.md added 33 micro-chapters to Parts D to F and ten recurring OCs (Human_Characters.md, Section 8A). Open: Part G has no slots for the follow-ons of Haddad, Hale, and Voss, whether any recurring OC survives into the 2160 first phase, and Reyes's Primaris date.

## 8. The Reapers

- Their FTL speed is 30 ly per day (author).
- They usually clean up the galaxy after each cycle so the next cannot discover how they operate.
- They know Arcturus is a stepping stone toward many unconnected systems. (open) Whether this holds after the Imperium moves the Arcturus relays.
- They search unconnected systems for developing species, but only within one day of travel from the relay network, and only after they finish wiping out the galaxy for the cycle.
- Kenji does not use Cerberus's Reaper research (author ruling), so it adds no indoctrination risk to his people.

**Derived from the Reaper speed**
- The Reaper search radius around the relay network is about 30 ly. One 30 ly sphere holds about 300 star systems, and the 25 mapped relay systems hold roughly 5,000 within 30 ly of any of them (range 4,000 to 7,500). The 15 ly spheres of the 25 relay systems hold roughly 900.
- Mark I warp (31.25 ly per day) is faster than the Reapers, as is every higher level. The Redoubt Worlds lie 1,000 ly from the nearest relay, far outside the radius.
- Moved relays remain in the relay network, and the Imperium still occupies the 15 ly around former Relay systems (author ruling).
- With every Imperial relay in Adamant, the radius around Imperial relays centers on Adamant. Moved relays relink to their former partners and open from Adamant.
- The Reaper radius stays at 30 ly (author ruling). The ring from 15 to 30 ly around Asgard, Utopia, Vetus, and the Fortress Systems is searched and unoccupied. Arcturus and Euler lie wholly inside the 100 ly sphere around Sol.
- (open) How continuous expansion changes the Reapers' search and cleanup.
