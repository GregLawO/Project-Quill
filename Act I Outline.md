# Act I Outline: The Gift

Plans A2, the chapter being written now, and A3, the next chapter. A1 is published and never rewritten. No chapter after A3 is outlined. Each later chapter is outlined, and any older sketch of it deleted, only when the story reaches the chapter before it (author ruling, 4 November 2026). Endings, batch lines, echoes, and wants are proposals (decision).

## Chapter by chapter

**Key.** Type: [I] investigative, [A] action, [H] hybrid. Ending (End): P physical event, Q quiet reversal, B held breath, W withheld answer, E emotional beat. Batch line: what a batch of five ends on emotionally. Echo: the earlier element a batch returns to. Wants: the small want and deadline given to each exposition narrator.

### Movement 1: The Three Doors

A1 (the body) is live and unchanged. Everything below it is open.

**The A2 plan in plain terms (author ruling).**

1. **The problem (2051).** Kenji sees that selling his inventions will make him powerful enemies, and even cost him his life. Before he sells anything, he builds a safety net.
2. **The Level 2 Med-Pod (2051).** It heals a dying person fully. It also rejuvenates, and it slows aging for a lifetime. Only Level 2 does any of this. A cruder version, the Primitive grade, cures disease and injury.
3. **The protectors (summer 2051 to 2055).** He finds veterans and experts willing to serve him for life, a life given for a life, in exchange for being restored to health or to their prime. Before they agree, he tells them about a loyalty clause that keeps them from telling outsiders what they do. The order is an author ruling: Vance (summer 2051), then Ashby, then the lawyers (October 2051), then a larger team, then Byron (July 2052). Whitcombe joins in February 2053. The Med-Pod does not exist before 2051, so recruitment starts then (decision).
4. **The fronts (summer 2053).** Three clinics, through a charity foundation called Tallis Bay: **Galveston, Marseille, and Singapore** (decision). The public floors run the Primitive pods that Oyama makes, and patients are asked to keep the true process secret. The hidden wings run Level 2, for recruits and later for paying elites. **The fronts also offer cloned organs for sale, pre-approved through the network's political faction.** That is why their paperwork is real.
5. **The elites (from spring 2055).** When the protectors are ready, he sells health and youth to people who will pay a great deal for them, through Whitcombe's factions, and he uses the sales to buy favor and influence. Politicians and public figures get health and a long life but not youth, because an old politician who suddenly looks thirty would draw questions. Rich private clients who can step out of public life get full rejuvenation (decision). Every buyer is made to understand, without a word said, that he holds the bigger stick: his men, his guards, and the loss of the treatment if they double-cross him.
6. **The Shepards (2053).** One of his men, Danny Shepard, asks him to save Danny's dying adoptive father, Tom. This is how Kenji meets the Shepards.
7. **Time passes (2053 to 2058).** The organization grows. He gets to know Hannah, they marry in September 2058, and he spreads his inventions through Oyama and through legal and shell companies.
8. **Good protection is not complete safety.** His people are human and make mistakes. A dedicated enemy who is willing to take losses can still reach him and the people he loves.

**The secret war (2055 to 2061).** Cerberus is called by its name or "they," never "it."

1. **A2.1 Kenji (31), Texas City, 2051 [I] · B. The Med-Pod, the problem, and the plan.** About 1,000 words, first person. He builds no Primitive pod.
   - **Opening detail.** He is seating the last gauge on the finished Level 2 Med-Pod in the back bay of Delgado's tire shop when Delgado comes in through the back door with his injured dog in his arms. The build diagram is pinned on the wall above the machine with MED-POD across the top.
   - **The build (Slow Gear, short).** The machine is a long steel cradle under a clear lid, with a tank made from a scrapped pressure vessel. Almost all of the parts came from a scrapper he built, and his yard wages paid for the rest. It is exactly the Med-Pod from the film Elysium. He knows how it works down to every weld and every setting, but he does not know how or why he knows. He woke in March 2050 in a flat three streets away, in a thirty-year-old body that was not his, and told no one. A few months later he built an automated scrapper and left it in a junkyard outside town to collect metal and parts. Next he built a Phased Disassembler Array, a very crude version that takes a long time to break scrap down into its base elements, and then the tools that make the tools for everything else in his head. Last, he rented the bay from Delgado and built the pod. No Reaper thought and no thought that this world was once a story.
   - **The test.** Delgado's dog Pancho was hit by a truck on the ship channel road that morning and has a hip broken in two places. The vet wants twenty-two hundred dollars or will put him down, and Delgado has neither. Kenji tells him to keep the hip level and that the cycle takes ninety minutes. The dog walks out of the pod and runs the length of the bay. Delgado asks nothing about the machine and thanks him at the door.
   - **The problem.** After Delgado leaves, Kenji washes his hands twice and looks at the pod. It rebuilds a body from the inside, whatever shape the body is in, and with the dials reversed it makes a person younger. Every government, hospital chain, drug company, and criminal who hears of it will want it. Delgado did not ask, but someone will, and whoever finds out will take the pod and the man who built it.
   - **The plan.** He takes the diagram down and writes on the back. Step one: protectors, people who can guard him and each other against legal and illegal threats. Step two: a factory. He will pay for protectors with the pod: anyone dying or broken comes out whole and swears themselves to him in exchange. During the treatment he will add a failsafe that guarantees their loyalty. Under the plan he draws two columns. Primitive is what he intends to share with the world, and Level 2 is what he keeps. The pod on his bench is Level 2. It heals a dying person completely, makes them young again, and slows their aging for half a lifetime. The Primitive version is a cruder machine that cures disease and injury, one the world could have built without him. He will not build it himself. He pins the sheet back on the wall with the plan facing out.
   - **The terms.** Service is for life, a life given for a life (author ruling, 4 November 2026).
   - **Hook (B).** The first name on his list is Dominic Vance, a retired Marine gunnery sergeant who trained half the reservists in Galveston County and is dying of ruined lungs in a Houston hospice. A volunteer at the veterans' hall gave him the name in the spring. On the first Monday of July 2051 he stands in the corridor outside Vance's room, with a rented van and a gurney at the curb. In a minute he will ask a dying man to trust a stranger with what is left of his life. *Tech: Med-Pod L2.*

2. **A2.2 Helen Marchetti (52), Houston, 2051 [I] · Q. The lawyers and Tallis Bay.** About 900 words. (New main cast, placeholder name. Habit of attention: who signs, and who can be sued.)
   - **Scene 1, October 2051, a Houston hospital room.** A man she has never met stands at the foot of her bed with no paper, no folder, no phone, and no pen. He and his two men walked past the nurses' desk without signing in and nobody questioned it. She has failing kidneys, and her partner Samuel Ortega, in a wheelchair with an oxygen line, has heart disease. One man stands in the doorway in an overcoat too warm for the ward and watches the corridor. The other carries a black case the size of a toolbox and watches the window. Kenji Himura offers to heal both of them, and says what he wants in return is their expertise and their loyalty. She asks who his friends are. He names Dominic Vance and Teodor Ashby, who keep him safe, and says the threat is people who would want what he has. He found them because both went on medical leave from the same firm in the same month, and the firm builds charitable foundations. He needs several in different countries, to own the things he will design, so that anyone who sues him has to go through the foundations. She asks who owns them and who signs. No one owns them, he says, and a trustee signs. He gives the name "Tallis Bay," and she writes it down without looking up. Before they sign, he tells them about a session that teaches them what they will need to know and also stops them from telling anyone outside the organization what it does or who runs it. They will still know it but will not be able to say it, and he cannot change that. Helen calls it a clause she cannot read, amend, or waive. He tells her because he would rather she heard it from him than found it later. Ortega, whose daughter turns nine in March, tells her to sign. She writes down that she was told first, dates it, and hands it to Kenji, who initials it.
   - **Scene 2, December 2051, the firm after hours.** The foundation papers are due at the state office on the thirty-first, or the first gift cannot be accepted tax-free this year. On the twentieth she is still at her desk at eight in the evening, six weeks off dialysis. Kenji arrives at nine with the same two men and reads the seven-year contract standing up. He stops at the clause that keeps treatments going for as long as someone serves.
   - **Hook (Q).** She asks what happens to everyone's term if he dies, because someone has to hold every term and treatment after him. He says Tallis Bay holds them and asks her to be its trustee. She tells him a trustee can refuse him and stop the foundation. He says he knows, and that he chose her because she asked about his death before she asked about the money, and because she will read a page to the end before she stops him. She writes *trustee* in the margin beside her own name. *Tech: Med-Pod L2.*

3. **A2.3 Byron Pyrrus (68), July 2052 [I] · Q. The producer.** About 800 words. (New main cast, placeholder age. Habit of attention: what a line can build by Friday, and what it cannot.)
   - **Scene (Slow Gear), a private hospital room.** Byron owns and runs Oyama Industries, a manufacturing group with nine thousand workers, and he is dying of lung cancer. Vance and four fighters, whom Vance recruited this spring, wait in the corridor. Kenji offers treatment and Byron's prime years back. In return Kenji gets Oyama: Byron's death is staged, he returns in a new identity, and he runs Oyama for Kenji (author ruling, 4 November 2026). Oyama builds what Kenji designs, under no name but Tallis Bay. Byron asks who owns the designs and what happens to his workers if Kenji disappears. Kenji says the designs belong to a foundation that cannot disappear, and that the contracts keep his workers paid.
   - **The terms.** Oyama builds industrial versions of Kenji's Phased Disassembler Array, the Resource Collector technology from Homeworld (author ruling, 4 November 2026). Kenji's own array is a very crude version that works slowly, and Oyama's are the top-of-the-line version. It vaporizes target matter and breaks it down to its base components, then separates and sorts the chemical elements by atomic mass, so no manufacturing waste is left. The organization will buy scrap and trash from across the whole country, and the arrays will break it down in a very short time. That is why it will never be short of materials. Oyama also builds the Primitive Med-Pods, Level 1 robots, and Level 1 powered-suit frames first, and later Level 2 and 3 robots. Kenji also hands Byron the security-droid design for Oyama's internal use, with a rule: not for sale. Kenji will supply the data crystals and the Dumb AI himself. Byron asks why. Kenji says the first buyer who learns whose design it is will try to take it.
   - Want: Byron's signature before the board meets on Monday.
   - **Hook (Q).** Byron signs. He says the droid drawings will go in his own safe, and not in the plant's. *Tech: Phased Disassembler Array (industrial), Med-Pod L1 (Oyama), Robotics L1, MIPS L1, Robotics L2 (design).*

4. **A2.4 Kenji (32), October 2052 [H] · Q. The protectors.** About 1,000 words, two dated scenes.
   - **Scene 1, a rented warehouse east of Houston (Slow Gear).** Fifteen people are in service for life: the two lawyers, Vance and seven fighters, three intelligence staff, and Ashby and one more communications engineer. The newest fighter is Danny Shepard, restored in September, a sapper who stands with his palm flat on a roof post. The fighters wear Level 1 frames from Oyama. Kenji cannot pay for more, and the Level 2 suit needs a mini-reactor he cannot afford. One squad of four Level 2 security droids, built by Oyama to his design and running his data crystals, stands by the wall. They are loud and they clank. Kenji asks what each machine is for.
   - **Told in one passage.** The hospice. Vance, dying, had nothing to pay with but what was in his head. Kenji copied his memories, experience, and expertise out and gave him his lungs back. The droids' fighting routines come from that copy. When a droid pins a training dummy, Kenji recognizes the way Vance would do it.
   - **Scene 2, a back bench.** Two frames lie half built. They will be his personal guards and will look like men. He is building them himself with money from Oyama, and they will take three years. Until they are done, Vance and Ashby walk with him.
   - **Hook (Q).** He writes 2055 in his notebook. He will sell nothing to anyone until the platoon is full, the droid squads are in place, and every site has a guard. *Tech: MIPS L1, Robotics L2, L5 (in progress), Dumb AI L2, Data Crystal.*

5. **A2.5 Eleanor Whitcombe (60), February 2053 [I] · Q. The political arm.** About 800 words. (New main cast, placeholder name and age. Habit of attention: she watches who looks at whom before they speak.)
   - **Scene (Slow Gear), the Houston warehouse.** She is a senator who chairs the committee that licenses medical devices, and she is hiding a failing heart. Marchetti, an old colleague, brought the invitation. Kenji's intelligence team found her heart records. She sees how the people in the room look at Kenji and at each other. She joins on the same terms as every recruit: service for life.
   - **The offer.** Health and a long life now. Youth later, "in ten years, when no one will ask." In return she leads the organization's political factions worldwide: introductions, her committee asking questions before anyone closes a clinic, and pre-approval for the cloned organs the fronts will sell. No one mentions a threat.
   - Want: her answer before her committee votes on Thursday.
   - **Hook (Q).** She says yes, and her first act is to ask for the list of cities where the clinics will open. Kenji says Galveston, Marseille, and Singapore. *Tech: Cloning L1, Med-Pod L2.*
- Batch ends on: a man who wants to stay unknown learning how many people it takes to protect him.

Danny asks that his family not learn where he works, and Kenji says he cannot stop Danny from being asked, only from lying.    - **The morning.** Kenji watches the clinic floor on a monitor, with Hannah in the waiting room. 7. **A2.7 Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance (placeholder), 2051 to spring 2059 [I] · Q. The network grows and Cerberus appears.** About 1,200 words. (Locked recurring OC, main cast. Told as his own ledger of dated entries. Habit of attention: what can be defended and what cannot. He reads ground, cover, and sight lines, and counts nothing.)
   - **Summer 2051.** He is treated, gives up his memories, and starts the network. His first act is to recruit Ashby, because someone must hold the radio.
   - **Nov 2052.** His rule: someone must watch the road beyond the fence. Danny Shepard reads a building by where it carries its weight, so Vance posts him to Galveston as site engineer and guard lead for the hidden wing.
   - **Summer 2053.** Three clinics open: Galveston, Marseille, and Singapore. The platoon grows to forty by autumn. Danny is moved to Houston in 2054, because Tom will be running the Galveston building and Danny cannot be seen by him.
   - **Apr 2055.** Whitcombe's factions bring the first elite buyers. Nobody has to be shown a threat, because the guards are in the room. The two Level 5 guards are finished this spring, and Vance stops walking behind Kenji.
   - **Nov 2055.** A client's physician asks a network nurse how the treatment works and offers money. The nurse reports it. The doctor leaves the country, and no one learns who his buyer was.
   - **Jun 2057.** Five Level 2 pods are stolen from a truck moving between clinics. The transfer order was forged. Droids do not ride on public roads, so only human guards were with the load.    - **Jan 2058.** Oyama's Primitive pods go on public sale. A law firm acting for a buyer no one can name offers to buy Oyama's technology patents. The man who runs Oyama, once Byron Pyrrus, refuses and calls Kenji.

   - **Hook (Q).** He writes the next job on a new page: find out who leads Cerberus. He has a name and no face, but he does not know where to start. *Tech: Robotics L2 and L3 (human guards beside them), Robotics L5, Med-Pod L2 wing.*

8. **A2.8 Hannah (23 and 25), Galveston, 2054 and 2056 [I] · P. The first answer, the first no, and the shakedown.** About 850 words.
   - **Opening detail.** The man across the table finishes everything on his plate, including the parsley.
   - **Scene 1, November 2054, her parents' kitchen (Slow Gear).** Sunday dinner. She is a Navy lieutenant home on leave. A fifth place is set and empty. Her parents say her brother Danny is working in Houston for a security contractor. She asks Kenji what he does when he is not at the clinic. He says, "I build things." She asks what things, and he says, "Tools, mostly." It is a true answer and a small one. He clears the plates and squares the stack. She leaves not liking him, but he has not lied to her.
   - **Scene 2, March 2056, the clinic (Slow Gear).** She left the Navy this spring, and her father talked her into the security post. Three weeks in, she walks the building with Kenji and lists what is missing: the license, the name tags, the locked wing, two grey frames the size of a man in the back corridor (her father calls them "equipment"), a door crew better trained than a clinic would pay for, and the two quiet men who follow Kenji from room to room and never sit down. He says yes or no to each. She asks what the grey frames are, and he says, "I can't answer that." She asks who the two men are. He says, "They're my guards." She sees it is true and sees that he will say no more. She says, "Which of these can you explain?" He says, "None of them, to you, yet." She says, "Yet." He lets her retrain the door crew her way. That evening she writes the first line on her list: why he stands at the edge of every room.
   - **Scene 3, a Tuesday in June 2056, the back corridor (Fast Gear).** Men with a letter from a hospital chain arrive with a tool bag. The chain has been losing patients to the charity floors, so these men are ordinary hired muscle. Her father is fixing a valve. Hannah steps between the men and the wing door and tells them to leave. At the far end Kenji stands with one hand on the door frame, doing nothing, and his two guards stand still beside him. She takes it for fear. A man takes a pipe from the bag.
   - **Hook (P).** Her father's hand goes to his back pocket. The pipe comes up. Servos shriek behind her. A metal hand closes around the hand that holds the pipe, and a second metal hand catches the pipe. The men are on the floor and tied, and two Level 2 droids stand over them. 9. **A2.9 Kenji (37), Galveston, April 2057 [I] · Q. The kidney and the apology.** About 900 words, two scenes, first person.

10. **A2.10 Hannah (26), Galveston port and a restaurant, autumn 2057 [I] · E. The weight and the first dinner.** About 900 words.

He says the arm does in forty minutes what his crew of eight did in a day, and the crew is down to three. The sled lifts two tons, but only for twenty minutes before the battery is flat. He needs her signature on his tally before the shift whistle. Kenji phones and asks what she needs. She says, "A weight." He says, "Ask me tonight."

It is the first time she has been allowed to fix his paper. He says, "Whoever took the pods filled in everything but the weight. Check it every time." She asks: "Do you want me to stop asking? I will, if you say so." He says, "No. When you stop asking, I won't know what you think." She says, "I think you are hiding something large, and not for money." He says, "No, not for money." She says, "Then I can work with that. Have dinner with me, somewhere that isn't my mother's table."
    - **Scene 3, Thursday, a diner on Seawall Boulevard (Slow Gear).** He asks her a question about herself, the first one he has ever asked: was it hard to leave the Navy? She talks for forty minutes. At her car he repeats back the name of her first ship and her old commanding officer. Nobody ever remembers those. At the corner table two men in plain jackets have had coffee in front of them for forty minutes and have not touched it. She takes them for his security.
    - **Hook (E).** She kisses him once, briefly. He stands there with his hands at his sides and says, "What do you need?" She laughs so hard she has to lean on the car, and says, "That. Again, Thursday."
